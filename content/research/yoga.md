# Yoga

## Summary
Yoga is framed as a psychological and physiological system, not just exercise. Asana, breath,
and ethical practices regulate the nervous system and improve circulation (blood + lymph).

## Key frameworks
- Eight limbs (Ashtanga):
  1) Yamas: ethical guidelines
  2) Niyamas: personal disciplines
  3) Asana: posture for stability
  4) Pranayama: breath regulation
  5) Pratyahara: sensory withdrawal
  6) Dharana: concentration
  7) Dhyana: meditation
  8) Samadhi: absorption

## Lymphatic support (mechanisms)
- Muscle pump: dynamic movement compresses lymph vessels.
- Inversion: gravity-assisted drainage.
- Compression/twists: “wring and soak” effect for abdominal organs.
- Diaphragmatic breath: pump effect for lymph movement.

## Lymphatic sequence (template)
- Neck & shoulder release
- 3–5 rounds of Sun Salutations
- Dynamic Cat–Cow
- Wide-legged forward fold
- Twisting chair
- Legs-up-the-wall
- Savasana

## Mental health + dosha sequences
- Vata (anxiety/overwhelm): slow, warming, grounding.
  - Child’s pose, Tree, Warrior II, Seated forward fold, long holds.
  - Pranayama: Nadi Shodhana.
- Pitta (anger/burnout): cooling, expansive, non-competitive.
  - Moon salutations, Cobra, twists, forward folds, supported bridge.
  - Pranayama: Sheetali.
- Kapha (lethargy/depression): stimulating, heating, uplifting.
  - Sun salutations A & B, Warrior I, Camel, Breath of Joy.
  - Pranayama: Kapalabhati.

## Desk yoga (5-minute protocol)
- Seated neck rolls
- Seated cat–cow
- Seated spinal twist
- Seated pigeon
- Wrist stretches
- Forward fold

## Content hooks
- Pair sequences with time-of-day or dosha guides.
- Match pranayama to emotional tone.
- Link to chakra support (e.g., heart-opening for heart chakra).

## Cautions & framing
- Emphasize accessibility and modification.
- Avoid medical claims; present as supportive practices.
