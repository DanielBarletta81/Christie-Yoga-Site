# Ayurveda

## Summary
Ayurveda frames health as dynamic balance among three bio-energies (doshas) derived from the
five elements. The goal is to align the current state (vikriti) back to the birth constitution
(prakriti) through food, routine, and seasonal adaptation.

## Key frameworks
- Tridosha theory: Vata (ether/air), Pitta (fire/water), Kapha (earth/water).
- Five elements: Ether, Air, Fire, Water, Earth.
- Prakriti vs. Vikriti: constitution vs. current imbalance.
- Ritucharya: seasonal regimen aligned to dominant dosha shifts.
- Dinacharya: daily routine aligned to circadian rhythms (e.g., finish dinner early; sleep before the 10pm–2am Pitta phase).

## Dosha profiles (template-ready)
- Vata (movement / kinetic energy)
  - Qualities: dry, light, cold, rough, subtle, mobile, clear.
  - Governance: respiration, heart pulsation, nerve impulses, elimination; sits in colon, pelvic cavity, bones, skin, ears, thighs.
  - Balanced: creativity, enthusiasm, flexibility, quick comprehension.
  - Excess: anxiety, insomnia, fear, scattered thoughts, constipation, dry skin.

- Pitta (transformation / metabolism)
  - Qualities: hot, sharp, light, liquid, spreading, slightly oily.
  - Governance: body temperature, vision, hunger, thirst, skin complexion; small intestine, stomach, sweat glands, blood, eyes.
  - Balanced: intelligence, leadership, decisiveness.
  - Excess: inflammation, acid reflux, rashes, burning sensations, anger, jealousy.

- Kapha (cohesion / structure)
  - Qualities: heavy, slow, cool, oily, smooth, dense, soft, stable, cloudy.
  - Governance: lubrication, immunity, strength/stamina; chest, throat, head, stomach, lymph, fat.
  - Balanced: love, patience, forgiveness, stability.
  - Excess: lethargy, weight gain, congestion, depression, attachment.

## Seasonal alignment (Ritucharya)
- Late winter (Kapha/Vata): cold/damp; mucus accumulation, stiff joints, strong appetite.
- Spring (Kapha): wet/heavy; liquefaction of kapha; allergies, congestion.
- Summer (Pitta): hot/dry; heat accumulation; dehydration; reduced digestion.
- Late summer/monsoon (Vata): humid/acidic; Vata aggravation; lower immunity.
- Autumn (Pitta -> Vata): cooling/dry; release of heat; increasing dryness/wind.
- Early winter (Vata): cold; consolidate warmth; build strength with routine.

## Content hooks (pair with yoga, recipes, rituals)
- Vata: grounding foods + stabilizing poses + warm routines.
- Pitta: cooling foods + slower pacing + calming breath.
- Kapha: stimulating movement + light meals + energizing rituals.

## Cautions & framing
- Keep tone supportive and non-prescriptive; emphasize self-observation and gentle adjustment.
- Avoid diagnosing; focus on lifestyle suggestions and options.
