# Research templates

These files are structured summaries mined from `Wellness App Content Outline.pdf`.
Use them as *content templates* for the creator to pair practices, videos, and guidance with
clear themes, rituals, and evidence framing.

Template fields used across files:
- Summary: short, neutral overview
- Key frameworks: definitions + conceptual model
- Practices & protocols: sequences, routines, or rituals
- Signals & imbalances: signs to look for
- Cautions & safety: contraindications or safety notes
- Linking ideas: how to connect yoga/ayurveda/chakra/crystal content

Source: Wellness App Content Outline.pdf (internal)
