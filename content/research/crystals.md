# Crystals / Lithotherapy

## Summary
Lithotherapy uses mineral structures as a symbolic and vibrational support system. In this
framework, stones are chosen by chakra color/frequency and used for ritual, grounding, and
intention work.

## Key principles
- Crystals exhibit piezoelectric and pyroelectric effects (charge under pressure/heat).
- Use color/vibration correspondences to match chakras.

## Chakra stone correspondences
- Root (red/black): Red Jasper, Black Tourmaline, Smoky Quartz.
- Sacral (orange): Carnelian, Moonstone.
- Solar Plexus (yellow): Citrine, Tiger’s Eye.
- Heart (green/pink): Rose Quartz, Malachite, Green Aventurine.
- Throat (blue): Sodalite, Aquamarine.
- Third Eye (indigo/purple): Amethyst, Lapis Lazuli.
- Crown (white/clear/violet): Clear Quartz, Selenite.

## Crystal elixirs (gem water) – safety protocols
Direct method (safe stones only):
- Safe: Quartz family (clear, rose, smoky, citrine, amethyst), Carnelian, Red Jasper, Aventurine.
- Cleanse stone, place in filtered water, infuse 4–12 hours, then remove stone.

Indirect method (toxic/fragile/soluble stones):
- Place stone in sealed glass vial; place vial in water; water touches glass only.
- Toxic/unsafe for direct water contact: Malachite, Lapis Lazuli, Tiger’s Eye (raw), Turquoise,
  Selenite, Fluorite, Moonstone/Labradorite, Galena, Cinnabar.

## Sun sensitivity
- Fade in sun: Amethyst, Rose Quartz, Citrine, Fluorite, Aquamarine, Kunzite, Celestite.
- Sun safe: Clear Quartz, Carnelian, Jasper, Black Tourmaline, Labradorite.
- Best practice: cleanse with moonlight, smoke, or sound for sensitive stones.
