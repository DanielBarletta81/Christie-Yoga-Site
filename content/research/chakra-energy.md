# Chakra / Energy

## Summary
Chakras are mapped as energy centers along the spine (pranamaya kosha) and linked with
emotional themes, elements, and daily rituals. Nadis (Ida, Pingala, Sushumna) are the channels
that carry energy through the subtle body.

## Subtle body anatomy
- Sushumna: central channel up the spine.
- Ida: left/lunar, cooling, parasympathetic.
- Pingala: right/solar, heating, sympathetic.

## Seven chakra overview (template-ready)
- Root (Muladhara): Earth. Themes: safety, grounding, survival. Imbalance: fear, insecurity.
- Sacral (Svadhisthana): Water. Themes: creativity, emotion, pleasure. Imbalance: guilt, numbness.
- Solar Plexus (Manipura): Fire. Themes: willpower, action, metabolism. Imbalance: anger, shame.
- Heart (Anahata): Air. Themes: love, grief, connection. Imbalance: loneliness, unforgiveness.
- Throat (Vishuddha): Ether. Themes: truth, communication. Imbalance: fear of speaking, tension.
- Third Eye (Ajna): Light. Themes: intuition, insight, vision. Imbalance: brain fog, headaches.
- Crown (Sahasrara): Thought/Space. Themes: spirituality, connection. Imbalance: cynicism, disconnection.

## Weekly chakra rituals (energy calendar)
- Monday (Moon / Sacral): salt bath, journaling, nourishing meal; wear orange/silver; mantra “I feel. I flow.”
- Tuesday (Mars / Root + Solar): intense movement, hardest task, organize finances; wear red; mantra “I am. I do.”
- Wednesday (Mercury / Throat): write, speak, sing/chant; wear blue; mantra “I speak. I express.”
- Thursday (Jupiter / Third Eye + Solar): study philosophy, visualize goals; wear yellow/indigo; mantra “I see. I expand.”
- Friday (Venus / Heart): self-care, social connection; wear green/pink; mantra “I love. I accept.”
- Saturday (Saturn / Root + Third Eye): deep clean, long meditation, hike; wear black/dark blue/violet; mantra “I know. I am grounded.”
- Sunday (Sun / Crown + Solar): sun salutations, rest, community; wear gold/white; mantra “I am light.”
