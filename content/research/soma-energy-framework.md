# Soma Energy Framework (mined)

Source: `Soma-Energy_Framework.pdf` (internal)

## Core thesis
- Ayurveda, Yoga, Chakra systems, and crystal work are presented as intersecting layers of a single wellness model.
- Emphasis is on balance, seasonal alignment, and ritual as a steadying practice rather than performance.

---

## Ayurveda (backend data)
### Tridosha system
- Vata (ether/air): movement, nervous system, elimination.
  - Qualities: dry, light, cold, rough, subtle, mobile, clear.
  - Excess: anxiety, insomnia, fear, scattered thoughts, constipation, dryness.
- Pitta (fire/water): metabolism, transformation, digestion.
  - Qualities: hot, sharp, light, liquid, spreading, slightly oily.
  - Excess: inflammation, acid reflux, rashes, anger, jealousy.
- Kapha (earth/water): cohesion, stability, structure.
  - Qualities: heavy, slow, cool, oily, smooth, dense, stable.
  - Excess: lethargy, weight gain, congestion, attachment.

### Ritucharya (seasonal rhythm)
- Late winter: Kapha/Vata → cold/damp → mucus + stiffness.
- Spring: Kapha → wet/heavy → congestion/allergies.
- Summer: Pitta → hot/dry → heat accumulation/dehydration.
- Monsoon/late summer: Vata → humid/acidic → Vata aggravation.
- Autumn: Pitta → Vata → cooling/dry → increasing dryness.
- Early winter: Vata → cold → protect warmth, build strength.

## Chakra system (backend data)
### Subtle body channels
- Sushumna (central), Ida (left/lunar), Pingala (right/solar).

### Seven chakra correspondences (summary)
- Root (Muladhara): Earth — safety/grounding. Imbalance: fear/insecurity.
- Sacral (Svadhisthana): Water — creativity/emotion. Imbalance: guilt/numbness.
- Solar (Manipura): Fire — willpower/metabolism. Imbalance: anger/shame.
- Heart (Anahata): Air — love/connection. Imbalance: grief/loneliness.
- Throat (Vishuddha): Ether — truth/communication. Imbalance: fear of speaking.
- Third Eye (Ajna): Light — insight/vision. Imbalance: brain fog, headaches.
- Crown (Sahasrara): Space/Thought — meaning/spirituality. Imbalance: cynicism, disconnection.

### Weekly chakra rituals (energy calendar)
- Monday (Moon) — Sacral: salt bath, journaling, nourish.
- Tuesday (Mars) — Root/Solar: action, boundaries, finances.
- Wednesday (Mercury) — Throat: write/sing/speak.
- Thursday (Jupiter) — Third Eye/Solar: study, visualize.
- Friday (Venus) — Heart: self-care, connection.
- Saturday (Saturn) — Root/Third Eye: structure, long meditation, nature.
- Sunday (Sun) — Crown/Solar: sun salutations, rest, community.

### Elixirs (safety)

- Indirect only: Malachite, Lapis Lazuli, Tiger’s Eye (raw), Turquoise, Selenite, Fluorite, Moonstone/Labradorite, Galena, Cinnabar.

### Sun sensitivity
- Fade in sun: Amethyst, Rose Quartz, Citrine, Fluorite, Aquamarine, Kunzite, Celestite.
- Sun safe: Clear Quartz, Carnelian, Jasper, Black Tourmaline, Labradorite.
